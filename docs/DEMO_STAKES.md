# Example stakes for the demo

Stakes to post in each sample chat that the AI has real past data for, so its reasoning (and the
admin's pricing record at **Chain → pricing record**, or "Admin: the full prompt" on the stake page)
shows linked history instead of "nothing to go on".

For every stake below:

- **About** is the person to pick as the subject. Each of them has private profile answers (sleep,
  punctuality, marks, goals...) that go to the AI, and the chat's AI context lists their habits.
- **History** is what the AI finds in that chat: settled stakes on the same habit, counted the way
  your stake is worded. "Makes the last train" and "misses the last train" are the same question
  worded the other way round, and the AI now counts them reversed.
- **Opens near** is roughly where the stats model opens it on a fresh copy. Gemma can move away
  from it for a reason (and says why), and the live copy's history may differ by a few points.

Post it as anyone else in the chat (Ilyas, Aariz, Durukan, Barkin, or the admin in Friday Dinner
Crew). Whoever posts can only trade once a friend has, and the subject can't bet on a stake about
themselves. Typing it close to the wording below gives the strongest match, but the matching also
understands other words for the same thing (cancels / bails / doesn't turn up, falls asleep / nods
off, late / on time).

## What normal accounts see

Under the odds, every stake now says in one line why it opened where it did (no chat quotes, no
private answers). Anyone in the chat who holds nothing on it can press **Dispute these odds** once:
the AI looks at the same evidence again, may move the price by up to 15 points, and says whether it
changed it. That uses up the stake's only dispute, whatever the answer. The full reasoning, prompt
and history stay on the admin's pricing record, which also shows the second look.
