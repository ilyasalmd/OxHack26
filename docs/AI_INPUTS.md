# What you can set for the AI

The AI (Gemma) prices each stake from four things you control, plus the group's own history.

## 1. Everyone's "About you" answers (You > Privacy)

Only the AI reads these.

- **What do you study or do, and which year?** Helps price exams, grades and deadlines about you. Example: "Economics, 2nd year".
- **Your recent marks or predicted grade?** Exam and grade stakes start from what you actually get. Example: "Averaging 64, predicted a 2:1".
- **How punctual are you, honestly?** Helps price “late to” stakes. Choices: Always early, On time, Usually a bit late, Famously late.
- **When do you usually sleep and get up?** Helps price 9am, alarm and bedtime stakes. Example: "Asleep around 1am, up at 9".
- **How active are you?** Helps price gym, running and sport stakes. Choices: Most days, A few times a week, Now and then, Rarely.
- **One or two goals for this year?** Helps price long-term stocks about you. Example: "Land a summer internship, run a half marathon".
- **Where are you with jobs or internships?** Helps price interview, offer and hearing-back stakes. Example: "15 applications out, 2 first rounds".
- **What do you do for fun?** Helps with stakes about your hobbies. Example: "Football, gigs, cooking".
- **Three words friends would use for you?** Sets the general tone of odds about you. Example: "Chaotic, loyal, competitive".
- **Relationship status, only if you want to share** Only for stakes about dating. Skip it if you'd rather. Choices: Single, Seeing someone, In a relationship, Engaged or married.

## 2. The chat's AI context (chat settings)

One paragraph per chat about the group's routines and facts. What helps depends on the kind of stake, below.

## 3. The stake itself

Title, who it's about, the deadline, bet or stock, yes/no or a number with a line (e.g. "over 60 tickets"), and any details.

## 4. The chat

The phone picks up to 20 recent messages about that person or topic, hides names and contact details, and sends those.

## Per kind of stake

The AI sorts every stake into one of these and weighs it with that kind's playbook. It also uses the group's record for that kind ("7 of 10 came true") and the person's own record.

### Lateness

- Starting point with no history: 50% for a bet, 35% for a stock
- Profile answers it reads: How punctual are you, honestly? · When do you usually sleep and get up? · What do you study or do, and which year?
- Worth putting in the chat context: Where and when the regular plans happen (Friday dinner 7:30pm at Maya's); How each person gets there and how far; Known disruptions (tube closures, engineering works)
- Example stakes: "Jake is late to Friday dinner", "Jake is more than 15 minutes late to the cinema", "Sam makes his 9am on Monday"

### Dating and relationships
